# Conditional Expectation

Viewed before $Y$ is revealed, the conditional expectation of $X$ given $Y$ is itself a random variable — a function of $Y$:

$$\mathbb{E}[X\lvert Y]=g(Y)$$

The mechanics of conditioning on an event or on another random variable are covered in [Conditional Distributions](../part-03-random-variables/07-conditional-distributions.md).
