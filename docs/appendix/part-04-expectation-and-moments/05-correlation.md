# Correlation

The **correlation coefficient** standardizes the covariance by the two standard deviations:

$$\rho(X,Y)=\dfrac{\mathrm{cov}(X,Y)}{\sigma_X\sigma_Y}$$

$$-1\leq\rho\leq 1$$
