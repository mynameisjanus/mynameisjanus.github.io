# Law of Total Expectation

Also known as the **law of iterated expectations**:

$$\mathbb{E}\left[\mathbb{E}[X\lvert Y]\right]=\mathbb{E}[X]$$

The event-partition version (the total expectation theorem) appears in [Conditional Distributions](../part-03-random-variables/07-conditional-distributions.md).
