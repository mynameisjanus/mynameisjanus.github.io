# Part I — Mathematical Foundations

The mathematical language the rest of the appendix is written in: sets, counting, notation, limits, and enough linear algebra to handle covariance matrices. Nothing here is probabilistic — these are the tools the probability starts from.

## Topics

| Topic | Focus |
|---|---|
| [Sets and Functions](01-sets-and-functions.md) | Set operations, de Morgan's laws, and the vocabulary of collections |
| [Counting Principles](02-counting-principles.md) | The multiplication principle, permutations, and combinations |
| [Mathematical Notation](03-mathematical-notation.md) | Symbols and conventions used throughout the appendix — sums and products, set-builder notation, indicators, and asymptotic notation |
| [Sequences and Infinite Series](04-sequences-and-series.md) | Convergence of sequences and infinite series, including the geometric series |
| [Basic Linear Algebra Review](05-linear-algebra-review.md) | Vectors, matrices, and the operations needed for covariance matrices and linear models |
