# Part VIII — Stochastic Processes

Randomness indexed by time: arrival processes (Bernoulli, Poisson, renewal), Markov chains and their hidden-state extension, and the continuous-time processes — Brownian motion and its geometric variant — that underlie price models.

## Topics

| Topic | Focus |
|---|---|

| [Bernoulli Processes](02-bernoulli-processes.md) | Arrival counts and waiting times in discrete time |
| [Poisson Processes](03-poisson-processes.md) | Continuous-time arrivals with exponential interarrival times |
| [Renewal Processes](04-renewal-processes.md) | Arrival processes with general interarrival distributions |
| [Markov Chains](05-markov-chains.md) | Transition matrices, state classification, and steady-state behavior |

| [Brownian Motion](08-brownian-motion.md) | The continuous-time limit of random walks |
| [Geometric Brownian Motion](09-geometric-brownian-motion.md) | The standard model of multiplicative price dynamics |
| [Martingales](10-martingales.md) | Fair-game processes and the optional stopping theorem |
| [Random Walks](11-random-walks.md) | Sums of i.i.d. steps and their long-run behavior |
