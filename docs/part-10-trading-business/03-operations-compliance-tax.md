# Operations, Compliance, and Tax

!!! warning "Under development"
    This lesson is part of the course scaffold and is being actively written.
    The learning objectives and outline below define its final scope.

## Learning objectives

By the end of this lesson you will be able to:

- Implement a daily trade-reconciliation process against broker records, classify breaks, and resolve them systematically
- Calculate NAV for a trading entity, including position pricing, expense accruals, and fee crystallization
- Map the compliance obligations — registration thresholds, record-keeping, marketing rules — that apply to a trading business at a given jurisdiction and AUM level
- Identify the major tax considerations for trading entities and formulate the specific questions to bring to qualified professionals (orientation, not advice)

## Outline

1. Trade reconciliation — broker versus internal records, break classification, resolution
2. NAV calculation — pricing sources, accruals, fee crystallization
3. Broker and custody relationships — selection, negotiation, redundancy
4. Compliance obligations by jurisdiction — registration and exemption thresholds
5. Record-keeping and marketing rules — what must be retained and for how long
6. Tax considerations for trading entities — entity type, trader status, cross-border issues (general orientation, not advice)
7. The operations calendar — daily, monthly, and annual obligations on one page
